New in 0.5.4
• AutoFill is faster: it no longer downloads and decrypts your whole vault on every request, and it no longer rewrites the Safari/app suggestion index when nothing changed.
• AutoFill now works on your PassAuth web vault too: save a login for your server's address and it is suggested on the vault's sign-in page.
• A suggestion for a login you have deleted is now removed from the QuickType bar instead of showing an error.
• If PassAuth's data can't be read for a moment (for example right after the phone restarts), AutoFill says so instead of asking you to sign in again.
Please test: use AutoFill on a few sites and in apps; open your server's web vault in Safari and check the login you saved for it is suggested; delete a login and confirm its suggestion disappears.
