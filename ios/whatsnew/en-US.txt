New in 0.6.0
• No more duplicate logins. If PassAuth already has a login for a site, it is updated instead of saving a second one. This also works when the saved login has only a username or only a password: PassAuth offers "Update login?" and fills in the missing part.
• "Save in" when saving a login. Before you save, you can pick which of your logins for the site to update, or choose "New login". The right one is picked for you.
• Redesigned AutoFill sheets: sign in with a passkey (choose the account, then Sign in), save a passkey, save or update a login, unlock, and choose a login or verification code. Each one shows the site at the top, one clear main button and quieter secondary actions, and reads properly at large text sizes.
• Safer defaults with shared vaults: a new passkey or an updated login is only suggested for logins in your own vault. A login in a shared vault is shown with the vault's name and is never picked for you.
• The account menu now asks before logging out. Trash and delete confirmations appear next to the item they belong to.
• Refreshed "Set up PassAuth" screen, move-item sheet, and Generator ("Copy password" is the main button).
