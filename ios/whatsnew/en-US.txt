New in 0.15.10
• The "Get notified when you sign in on another device" card is gone from the vault. Sign-in request notifications are now turned on only with their switch in Settings → Account security; iOS asks for permission when you turn it on.

Please test:
1. Install fresh and sign in to a server that sends sign-in request notifications: the vault opens with no notifications card and no permission prompt.
2. Settings → Account security → Sign-in request notifications: turning the switch on shows iOS's notification prompt. After Allow, the switch stays on.
3. If you choose Don't Allow, the switch stays off and "Open the Settings app" appears below it.
