New in 0.9.0
• Add a site to a login you already have: when you use the same account on another site (for example your mail login on a developer console), PassAuth no longer needs a second copy. In the AutoFill list, tap "Add this site to a login…", pick the login and confirm: it is filled now and suggested on that site from then on.
• When you save a password that matches a login saved for another site, the save sheet also offers "Add this site" next to "New login".

Please test:
1. In Safari, open a site where your account works but nothing is saved for it yet. Open AutoFill, tap "Add this site to a login…", pick the login and confirm. Check it fills, and that next time it is suggested for that site.
2. Sign in on such a site with the same user name and password as an existing login and save: the sheet should offer "Add this site". "New login" stays selected until you choose otherwise.
