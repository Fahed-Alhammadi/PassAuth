New in 0.13.0
• Two-step login with a security key or passkey now works in the app. When you choose "Security key or passkey", PassAuth opens your server's page in a secure browser sheet; touch your key or use your passkey there, and the app finishes signing you in.
• Needs PassAuth server 0.16 or later for this. With an older server the app keeps offering your other two-step methods.

Please test:
1. Add a security key or passkey for two-step login in the web vault (Settings → Account security).
2. Sign out on this phone, sign in again and choose "Security key or passkey". Check the browser sheet opens, your key works, and you land in your vault.
3. Start again and tap Cancel or close the sheet; check you return to the choice of methods without an error.
