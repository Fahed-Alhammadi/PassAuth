New in 0.9.1
• Settings is reorganised: your account is at the top, then grouped rows with coloured icons (vault tools, security and AutoFill, appearance and permissions, About).
• Everything about your account is on one page: tap your name to see the server, switch or add accounts, lock, log out or delete the account.
• New About PassAuth page with the version, help, feedback, rating, website and privacy policy.
