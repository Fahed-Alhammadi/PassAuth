New in 0.11.0
• Sends with a password are now locked with that password: the link alone can no longer open the text, not even with the server's data. Your own devices still open and edit your Sends.
• PassAuth now notices if your server ever sends an older version of an item. It warns you on the item and keeps the newer copy when it has one.
• Personal vault keys are stored in a stronger format once every device on your account has this update.

Please test:
1. Create a Send with a password, open its link in a browser and check the text only appears after the right password.
2. Change the Send's password and remove it again; check the Send still opens each time.
3. Edit a few logins on this phone and on another device and check nothing shows an "older version" warning.
