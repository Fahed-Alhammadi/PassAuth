New in 0.15.8
• The "Set up PassAuth" screen on first launch is gone. The app opens straight on "Connect to your PassAuth server".
• iOS now asks for each permission only when you use the feature: the camera when you scan a QR code, the local network when you connect to a server at home (such as http://192.168.1.20), notifications, AutoFill and Face ID from their switches in Settings.

Please test:
1. Delete PassAuth and install this build: it opens on "Connect to your PassAuth server", with no permission request.
2. If your server is on your home network, tap Connect: iOS asks for the local network, and after Allow the app connects.
3. Add a verification code with "Scan QR code": iOS asks for the camera the first time.
