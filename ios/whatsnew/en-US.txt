New in 0.5.3
• Background sync: PassAuth now refreshes your offline copy of the vault in the background, about every 2 hours by default (iOS decides the exact moment), so it's current when you open the app or use AutoFill. Change or turn it off in Settings → Vault options → Background sync.
Please test: change a login on another device, leave the iPhone alone for a few hours with the app in the background, then open PassAuth and check the change is already there before the sync spinner runs.
