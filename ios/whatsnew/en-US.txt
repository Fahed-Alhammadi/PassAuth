New in 0.7.0
• Sign in with another device: on a new phone or computer, choose "Sign in with another device" and approve it on your iPhone. You pick the words the new device shows, then type the 6-digit code your iPhone shows on the new device.
• Faster sync: only what changed since the last sync is downloaded.
• Vault invitations (Settings): accept or decline when someone invites you to a shared vault.
• Password health (Settings): weak and reused passwords, and, when you tap "Check for exposed passwords", passwords found in known data breaches. Only the first 5 characters of each password's hash leave your iPhone, and only if your server allows the check.
• Signed-in devices (Settings → Account security): see where your account is signed in and sign out the devices you don't use.
• Better website matching: PassAuth now knows every public domain ending (like .co.uk), so AutoFill suggests the right logins on more sites.
• Your server's account policies (for example, required two-step login) are followed, and error messages are clearer.
Most new features need PassAuth server 0.10.0 or later.

Please test:
1. Open the web vault in a private browser window, choose "Sign in with another device", and approve it on your iPhone: pick the words the browser shows, then type the code on the browser.
2. Settings → Password health: open a weak or reused password from the list, then tap "Check for exposed passwords".
3. Settings → Account security → Signed-in devices: sign out the browser from step 1.
4. Use AutoFill on a few sites you use, including one with a country domain such as .co.uk.
