New in 0.8.0
• Send (Settings → Send): share a note through an encrypted link that expires. You choose how long it lasts, how often it can be opened, and an optional password.
• Attachments: add files and photos to an item, then open or share them from the item page.
• Emergency access (Settings → Emergency access): name someone you trust who can ask for view-only access to your personal vaults after a waiting period you choose. You can also be someone else's contact.
• Signed sharing: shared vaults are now checked against the signature of the person who shared them. A vault your server added without an invitation waits for your OK, and you're warned when someone's keys change. Your own fingerprint is in Settings → Account.
• Editing an item on your iPhone no longer drops its attached files or details saved by newer apps.
These features need PassAuth server 0.12.0 or later.

Please test:
1. Settings → Send: create a Send with a password and open its link in Safari.
2. Open a login, add a photo as an attachment, then open it again from the item.
3. Settings → Emergency access: add a second test account as a contact, confirm it after comparing fingerprints, then remove it.
4. If someone shares a vault with you: check that Vault invitations shows "Signed by" with their email, and compare the fingerprint with them.
