New in 0.14.0
• Sign-in request notifications: when you sign in on a new device with "Sign in with another device", your iPhone now gets a notification. Tap it, unlock PassAuth and approve the request as before (matching words and a 6-digit code). Notifications never show your email or server.
• Turn them on from the card in your vault, or in Settings → Account security → Sign-in request notifications.

Needs a PassAuth server 0.17.0 or later (with push notifications switched on in the admin page, the default).

Please test:
1. Tap "Turn on" on the vault card and allow notifications.
2. Lock PassAuth and close it. On a computer, open the web vault, choose "Sign in with another device" and type your email.
3. Check a "Sign-in request" notification arrives within a few seconds.
4. Tap it: PassAuth opens, asks you to unlock, then shows the request. Approve it and finish signing in on the computer.
