New in 0.10.0
• Profile picture: tap the circle on the Account page (Settings → your name) to choose a photo or remove it. It is encrypted before it leaves your phone and appears on your other devices. Without a photo your initials show as before.
• With website icons turned off, logins now show a globe instead of the first letter of their name.

Please test:
1. Settings → your name → tap the big circle → Choose photo… and pick a photo. Check it shows on the Account page, the Settings card and the account menu.
2. Open PassAuth on another device signed in to the same account and check the photo appears there after a moment.
3. Remove the photo and check your initials come back.
4. Turn website icons off in Settings and check the vault list shows globes.
