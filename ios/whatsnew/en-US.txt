New in 0.12.0
• PassAuth now keeps a record of the items in each vault and checks your server against it. If the server ever hides a change, brings back a deleted item or shows an older copy of a vault, you get a warning on that vault.
• After you restore your server from a backup, the vaults that changed show a warning. Tap "Use the server's copy" once per vault to accept what the server has now.
• Items that can't be opened are listed instead of hidden, and can be deleted. Items saved by a newer version of PassAuth are listed with a note to update.
• Items in the trash are removed by your apps when their time is up, never sooner than seven days after this device first saw them there.
• PassAuth now needs a server on version 0.12 or later, and tells you when a server is too old.

Please test:
1. Add, edit, trash and restore a few items on this phone and on another device; check that no warning appears.
2. Lock and unlock, and use AutoFill in Safari; check your logins are suggested as before.
3. If you share a vault, change an item from another account and check it arrives here without a warning.
