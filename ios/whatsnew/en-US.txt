New in 0.15.4
• New switch in Settings → AutoFill: "Suggest every login above the keyboard". Since 0.15.3, logins set to match by Host, Exact or Starts with (and logins that share a user name on one domain) are reached through PassAuth in the suggestions, so iOS can't offer the wrong one. If you prefer them directly above the keyboard again, turn this on. iOS may then suggest a login from another site of the same domain: check the site name before you tap it.

Please test:
1. With the switch off (the default), a login set to Host appears under PassAuth in the suggestions, not as its own suggestion.
2. Turn it on, then open that login's sign-in page again: it should appear directly above the keyboard.
