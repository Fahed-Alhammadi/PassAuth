New in 0.15.9
• Password health now catches common passwords. Passwords such as password123, P@ssw0rd! or Summer2024! count as weak, even when they mix letters, numbers and symbols. Random and generated passwords are rated as before.
• The strength meter for a new master password uses the same check.

Please test:
1. Save a login with the password password123, then open Settings → Password health: it is listed under Weak passwords.
2. A login with a password made by the generator is not listed as weak.
3. Settings → Account security → Change master password: typing Summer2024! as the new password shows Weak.
