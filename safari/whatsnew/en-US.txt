First test version of PassAuth for Safari.

PassAuth now works in Safari on iPhone, iPad and Mac:
- Fill logins from your PassAuth vault, with the menu inside login fields.
- Save new logins and update changed passwords when you sign in.
- Add a website to a login you already have.
- If the PassAuth app on the same device is unlocked, your server, email and Secret Key are filled in for you: just type your master password.

To turn it on: open the PassAuth for Safari app and follow the steps. Passkeys and AutoFill in other apps keep coming from the PassAuth app.

Please tell us what doesn't work, especially on websites where the menu doesn't appear or a login isn't saved.
